On **September 9, 2026**, developer **Timon Herzog** and publisher **Metaroot** announced the first major DLC for *The Farmer Was Replaced*: **The Miner Was Replaced**, with a release date of **November 2, 2026**.

Primary sources:

- [Steam news: The Miner Was Replaced releases November 2!](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2060160/view/687515890081597557)
- [Steam DLC store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4521700/The_Miner_Was_Replaced/)
- [Metaroot press kit](https://www.metaroot.ch/press-kit/the-miner-was-replaced)

## What the DLC adds

Official copy (paraphrased from Steam / press kit):

- Leave the farm for **underground mining** — dig, explore, and optimize routes in code.
- **New resources**, including coal, quartz, mushrooms, and dynamite (eight new underground resources in total).
- New programming problems: careful ruin excavation, **sorting colorful mushrooms**, **rice on clay**, and a **Minesweeper-inspired** challenge for the mining drone.
- A **new section of the unlock tree** on top of the base game.

The DLC requires the base game. Wishlist and any playtest signup are handled on Steam (the announcement invited players to sign up from the base-game / DLC pages).

## Base game platforms (as of Oct 2026)

Still relevant while you wait for the DLC:

- **Windows** and **macOS**: native Steam builds.
- **Linux** / **Steam Deck**: play via Proton; Deck listing is **Playable** (some text entry needs the on-screen keyboard).
- Not available as an official browser, Android, or iOS game.

## What this means for scripts on this site

Until November 2, the copy-paste pages here stay focused on the **base game**: pumpkin, cactus, sunflower, maze, dinosaur hat, trees, carrots, and beginner loops.

- Beginner maze left-wall followers remain valid on **fresh** mazes.
- Community leaderboard patterns from September 2026 (multi-drone maze packing, dead-pumpkin tracking, sunflower max-petal passes without shared drone state) are noted on the respective code pages — they do not replace the teaching scripts.
- We will add Miner-specific guides after the DLC is public and APIs / unlocks are stable. Do not expect mining scripts before then.
