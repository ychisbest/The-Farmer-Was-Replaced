## Official Steam vs. CD Key Sites: What to Know

| Buying Option | Official? | Key Risks &amp; Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Steam Store** (`app/2060160`) | Yes | Full refund policy, native Windows &amp; macOS, supports the developer directly |
| **Authorized Key Stores** (e.g. Humble, Fanatical when stocked) | Yes | Official keys sourced from publisher; check region lock before redeeming |
| **Gray-Market Key Resellers** (G2A, etc.) | No | Unverified sellers, no Steam refund eligibility, occasional revoked keys |

**Tip:** *The Farmer Was Replaced* is already an affordable indie title and goes on Steam seasonal sales regularly. Wait for a Steam sale or use SteamDB/GG.deals price history rather than risking an unverified gray-market key.

## Does a Steam Key Unlock Mobile or the Miner DLC?

- **Mobile (Android / iOS):** **No.** A PC Steam key does **not** unlock the mobile app on Google Play or the App Store — they are separate purchases. (Note: the mobile version includes *The Miner Was Replaced* DLC by default; see our [mobile guide](/blog/mobile-android-online/).)
- **The Miner Was Replaced DLC:** On Steam, the base game and the *The Miner Was Replaced* DLC are sold separately (or in a Steam bundle). Check the store page before buying a third-party key so you know whether it is base-only or bundled.
