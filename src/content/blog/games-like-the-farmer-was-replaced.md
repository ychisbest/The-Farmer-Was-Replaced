Finished the farm and want more code-driven automation? These are the games players recommend most often in the community, grouped by what you liked about **The Farmer Was Replaced**.

## Quick picks

| If you liked… | Play |
|---|---|
| More of the same, underground | **The Miner Was Replaced** (DLC) |
| Real code, open-ended | **Bitburner**, **Screeps** |
| Programming a farm / colony of bots | **Autonauts**, **Colobot** |
| Learning Python or JavaScript | **CodeCombat** |
| Short logic puzzles | **Human Resource Machine**, **7 Billion Humans** |
| Low-level / hardware | **Turing Complete**, **Shenzhen I/O**, **TIS-100**, **Exapunks** |

## 1. The Miner Was Replaced

The first DLC (November 2, 2026): same drone and language, now mining underground — coal, quartz, mushrooms, dynamite, ruins, mushroom sorting and Minesweeper. Needs the base game. [Our overview](/blog/the-miner-was-replaced/).

## 2. Bitburner

A free hacking-themed idle game where you write real **JavaScript** to automate everything. The closest match if you enjoyed optimizing scripts and watching numbers climb.

## 3. Autonauts

Build a colony and teach bots farming, crafting and building with a **visual block language**. The theme is almost the same — automating farm work with robots — with a gentler coding curve.

## 4. CodeCombat

A browser game that teaches **Python and JavaScript** by moving a hero through levels with code. Good for younger players or anyone who wants to turn the game's Python-like syntax into real Python.

## 5. Human Resource Machine

A short, funny puzzle game from Tomorrow Corporation: you program an office worker with assembly-like commands. Each level is a tiny algorithm, like the farm's early unlocks.

## 6. 7 Billion Humans

The sequel to Human Resource Machine: you program a whole crowd of workers at once — the "many drones in parallel" feeling from Megafarm.

## 7. Turing Complete

Build a computer from logic gates, then write assembly for it. Recommended in the community for players who liked the farm's puzzle side and want to go lower level.

## 8. Shenzhen I/O, TIS-100 and Exapunks

Zachtronics programming puzzles: tiny assembly languages, tight constraints and leaderboards for size and speed — the same "optimize your solution" itch as maze and cactus.

## 9. Screeps

An MMO where your units run your **JavaScript** around the clock. Very open-ended and long-term; for players who want automation that runs while they're away.

## 10. Colobot

An older (now open-source) game where you program robots in a C-like language to colonize a planet. Often mentioned as the game The Farmer Was Replaced reminds people of.

## Which one should you play first?

- Want something free and deep: **Bitburner**.
- Want the same cozy automation vibe: **Autonauts**.
- Want short puzzles: **Human Resource Machine**.
- Want more of this exact game: **The Miner Was Replaced**.

## FAQ

**Is The Farmer Was Replaced good for learning to code?**
Yes. The language is Python-like and introduces loops, functions and lists one at a time. CodeCombat is the best next step for real Python.

**What is the closest game to The Farmer Was Replaced?**
Its own DLC, The Miner Was Replaced. Outside of it, Autonauts (bot farming) and Bitburner (script automation).

**Are there free games like it?**
Bitburner is free on Steam and in the browser; CodeCombat has free levels.
